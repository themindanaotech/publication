Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

The Mindanao Tech is the student publication of the University of Southern Mindanao. This page explains what information we collect on this website and how we use it.

Reading the site

You can read every story without signing in or giving us any information.

Signing in with Google (comments and likes)

To like a story or write a comment, you sign in with a Gmail account through Google. When you do, we receive from Google:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile photo

We use your name and photo to show next to your comments. Your email address is used only to confirm that you are signing in with a Gmail account and to keep the comment section free of spam. It is never shown publicly. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail messages, contacts or any other Google data.

Your comments are public and show your name, photo and the date they were posted. You can delete your own comments at any time using the Delete button next to them.

Email subscriptions

If you subscribe for updates, we store the email address you enter so we can tell you when new stories are published.

Where your information is kept

Information is stored with our database provider, Supabase. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your account information, comments, likes or subscription removed, message us on our Facebook page and we will delete them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Message us on our Facebook page.
